# Video Motion Storyboard

Every scene declares id, start/end, narrative purpose, copy, composition type, layers, entrance/hold/exit motion, transition, preferred renderer, fallback renderer, assets and acceptance notes.

## Timing rules
- One scene communicates one primary idea.
- Keep Korean reading time realistic.
- Do not stack new copy and major camera motion on the same few frames unless intentional.
- Reserve transition overlap explicitly.
- Keep title-safe and action-safe margins.

## Visual grammar
Lock typography scale, spacing, corner treatment, stroke weight, shadow behavior, motion easing, transition family, background texture and accent behavior before implementation.

## Regeneration rule
If one scene fails, revise that scene contract first. Do not regenerate unrelated scenes unless shared timing or style changed.
