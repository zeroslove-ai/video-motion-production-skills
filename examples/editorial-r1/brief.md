# Editorial R1 Brief

## Topic
AI 3D 캐릭터 제작은 어떻게 빨라지는가

## Purpose
Explain the structural change from a long sequential production chain to a more agent-assisted, repeatable pipeline. This is a workflow demo, not a quantitative market claim.

## Audience
Non-developer decision makers who should understand the production logic in under 30 seconds.

## Look
Dark editorial canvas, warm white cards, electric accent, large Korean typography, diagrams, arrows and restrained depth.

## Required beats
1. Strong title
2. Conventional sequential pipeline
3. Agent-assisted pipeline
4. Human judgment vs machine repetition
5. Reusable motion-production architecture
6. R1 proof statement

## Constraints
- 30s
- 16:9
- 30fps
- no paid generation
- no external runtime assets
- all major text live
